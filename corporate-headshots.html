Common Inquiries

Corporate FAQs

Do we need to travel to your studio, or do you come to us?

+

Both options are available. For individual executives and small groups, my studio offers a dedicated, relaxed setting. For corporate teams of 5 or more, I regularly set up my mobile studio directly in your conference rooms or boardrooms.

What wardrobe guidance do you provide for business professionals?

+

I provide a concise prep guide in advance: dark navy, charcoal, or neutral jackets; structured necklines; avoiding small tight herringbone patterns that cause digital moiré. Ties or open collars can both be captured during the session.

How quickly are final images delivered?

+

The online proofing gallery is sent within 48 hours for your HR or comms team to select favourites. Final retouched high resolution assets are delivered within 5 working days.
