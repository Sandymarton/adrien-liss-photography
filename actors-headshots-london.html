Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does your acting background make a difference?

+

Most photographers simply shoot what’s in front of them. Having acted for over 20 years, I know how to direct your intentions, eye lines, and micro expressions, putting you at total ease so your personality cuts through the frame.

How long does a session last?

+

There is no rigid cutoff. Most sessions naturally conclude between 2 to 3 hours, giving you ample time for multiple outfit and lighting changes without rushing.

What should I wear?

+

Bring a wide selection of tops, layers, and necklines in solid, muted tones. I will evaluate your wardrobe with you before shooting to align with your casting types.

How does the retouching process work?

+

Casting directors need to see who walks into the room. Retouching cleans distractions, flyaway hairs, and temporary blemishes while keeping your natural skin structure 100% genuine.

Turnaround time?

+

Proof gallery in 48 hours; final retouched selections delivered within 5 to 7 working days.
