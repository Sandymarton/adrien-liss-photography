BEHIND THE LENS • ADRIEN LISS

Every portrait is an encounter between presence, technique, and genuine human truth.

With more than twenty years behind the lens, my relationship with image making began not in a technical studio, but in the theatre. My creative journey started in Rome through dedicated theatre and acting training. That foundation taught me to perceive what is unspoken: the subtle tension in an eye glance, the weight of posture, and how to create the quiet trust that allows people to drop their guard. Whether directing an actor preparing for a career-defining audition, an executive leadership board, or an international commercial campaign, I do not simply capture likenesses; I reveal character.

That same exacting discipline defines my work as an entrepreneur. With a foundation in economics, I founded Pure Ledger in partnership with Campus Bio-Medico University Hospital of Rome, co-founded the architectural design studio AsimmetrikA alongside Engineer Claudio Basile and Architect Simone Pinducciu, and develop AI automation solutions at Arrakis Technologies. For me, photography, architecture, and technology share the exact same craft: stripping away noise to reveal what is essential, commanding, and timeless.
